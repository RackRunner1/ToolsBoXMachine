Enable Incognito Mode?

When your browser session ends, all your notes and all note settings will be permanently deleted.

  • Closing the browser erases every note
  • Folders, word count and delete confirmation preferences are reset
  • Nothing can be recovered afterwards

Incognito Mode unavailable

A password is set on your notes. Incognito Mode can't be enabled while a password is set.

To use Incognito Mode, remove the password in the Privacy tab first.

Add a password

Your notes, folders and .tbxm exports will be encrypted with this password. If you forget it, your data cannot be recovered.

At least 4 characters, no spaces.

Your notes are stored locally

Your notes are saved only in your browser, not on our servers.

  • Clearing your browsing data (cache, cookies, local storage)
  • Switching browsers or devices
  • Using private / incognito browsing mode

In these cases, everything you have written will be permanently lost.
