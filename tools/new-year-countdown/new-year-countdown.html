How the New Year countdown works

This free New Year countdown shows the exact time left until January 1st at 00:00 in your local timezone. The timer updates every second and counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds until the new year begins, with a progress bar for the current year. It runs entirely in your browser, so there is no account to create and nothing on this page is tracked.

How many days until New Year?

The countdown above is the most precise answer: it uses the timezone reported by your device and counts down to the next January 1st, down to the second. The Days value is the number of whole days left, which is the number most people mean when they ask how many days until New Year.

Be careful with calendar days: a day is counted from midnight to midnight, so the number of whole days left can be one less than the difference between today's date and January 1st. In the last week of December the days value drops quickly, and on December 31st the timer shows hours, minutes and seconds only. For the exact moment, read all four values together.

New Year in other timezones

New Year does not begin at the same moment everywhere. Midnight on January 1st reaches the Pacific first and crosses the globe over about 26 hours. The countdown uses your device's timezone, so it always matches your local clock; this table shows when the new year starts in a few major cities, expressed in UTC.

Frequently asked questions

How many days until New Year?

The countdown above shows the exact number of days, hours, minutes and seconds until New Year in your timezone.

How many hours until New Year?

The timer counts the hours left after the whole days, so on December 31st the hours value is the one to watch. It updates every second, including the final countdown to midnight.

How long until New Year's Eve?

New Year's Eve is December 31st. This countdown is also the countdown to the end of New Year's Eve: when the timer reaches zero, it is midnight and the new year has begun.

What time does New Year start?

New Year starts at 00:00 on January 1st in your local timezone. Because the world uses different timezones, it happens at a different UTC moment in each country; the table above gives a few examples.

Does the countdown use my timezone?

Yes. The page reads the timezone reported by your browser and calculates the countdown from your device's clock. It does not ask for your location and does not send your timezone anywhere.

How many days since New Year?

For the first 48 hours after January 1st, the page switches to a "time since New Year" mode and shows how long ago the new year started. After that window it goes back to counting down to the next one.

Is the New Year countdown free and private?

Yes. It is free, has no ads and no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you do on the page is tracked.

Can I use the countdown fullscreen on a TV or at a party?

Yes. Use the fullscreen button in the top-right corner of the timer card. The countdown fills the screen and keeps updating, which works well on a TV, a projector or a second monitor.
