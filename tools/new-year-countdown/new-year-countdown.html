How the New Year countdown works

This free New Year countdown shows the exact time left until January 1st at 00:00 in your local timezone. The timer updates every second and counts down the days, hours, minutes and seconds until the new year begins. It runs entirely in your browser, so no account is needed and nothing you do on this page is tracked.

Time until New Year

From January 1st of the current year until the end of December 31st, the countdown shows how much time remains before midnight on New Year's Eve. The progress bar shows how much of the current year has already passed, as a percentage.

Time since New Year

For the first 48 hours after January 1st, the page switches to "time since New Year", so you can see how long ago the new year started. After that window, it goes back to counting down to the next New Year.

Why the timezone matters

New Year does not start at the same moment everywhere. Midnight on January 1st happens first on islands such as Kiribati and last in places such as American Samoa. This tool uses the timezone of your device, so the countdown always matches your local clock.

Frequently asked questions

  • How many days until New Year? The number of days is shown in the first box of the countdown above.
  • How many days since New Year? During the first 48 hours of the year, the tool shows the time elapsed since January 1st at 00:00.
  • Is this New Year countdown free? Yes. It has no ads, no sign-up, and no tracking.
  • Can I show the countdown on a big screen? Yes. Use the fullscreen button in the top-right corner of the timer card.
