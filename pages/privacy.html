Privacy Policy

Data Collection

ToolsBoXMachine runs on our own server, which we control ourselves. We do not log visits: we do not record IP addresses, user agents, referrers, requested pages or any other information about your passage on the site.

Like any web server, the page files are delivered to your browser when you open them, but nothing about that exchange is kept.

Our hosting provider, OVH, may log IP addresses at the network level for DDoS protection purposes. These logs are kept by OVH under its own control and are not accessible to us.

Client-Side Processing

Security is our priority. Unlike most online utilities, all tools available on TBXM are executed entirely within your browser.

None of the data you process is ever sent to our servers. Everything stays on your local machine, ensuring total privacy and maximum security.

Cookies

We use local storage only to remember your preferences (like the last tool used) for a better user experience. No tracking cookies are used.
