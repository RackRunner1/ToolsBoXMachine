About Us

ToolsBoXMachine is a free, open-source collection of browser-based utility tools designed for developers, students, and anyone who needs quick digital helpers. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no data is ever sent to a server, no tracking, no accounts required. Your privacy is fully respected.

Whether you need to blur sensitive parts of an image, generate a strong password, format JSON, create a QR code, or simply run a quick calculation, TBXM has you covered. Each tool is crafted to do one thing and do it well, following the Unix philosophy of simplicity and reliability.

Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no bloat — the site loads fast and works on any device. Maintained by RackRunner and licensed under the Unlicence, TBXM is community-driven and always improving.

History

ToolsBoXMachine was created on April 17, 2025. On September 6, RackRunner took over development from Drayko and became the new maintainer. We hope this project will live for a very long time, always growing with new tools and improvements for the community.

The Team

ToolsBoXMachine is developed by RackRunner, who took over as maintainer after months of initial development by Drayko. RackRunner continues to build new features, fix bugs and improve the experience for everyone.
